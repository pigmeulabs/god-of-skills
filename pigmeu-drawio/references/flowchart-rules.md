# Flowchart Rules

Use flowcharts for algorithmic logic, operational procedures, validation flows, and simple process explanations.

## Symbols

- Start/end: rounded terminator.
- Process: rectangle or rounded rectangle.
- Decision: diamond with a question label.
- Data/input/output: parallelogram when relevant.
- Connector: small circle only for long diagrams.

## Layout

- Use top-to-bottom for procedural flows.
- Use left-to-right for compact business flows.
- Keep the happy path centered.
- Put errors, denials, and exceptions to the right or bottom.
- Label decision exits with `sim`/`não`, `yes`/`no`, or domain-specific values.
